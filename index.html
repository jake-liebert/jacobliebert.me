01
2026 AI Agents · FP&A

FP&A AI Agent

Hybrid chat + agentic AI for Walmart Finance — launched 2026, adopted by 150+ users.

Problem

Walmart Finance analysts spent hours every week pulling data, running variance analyses, and stitching forecasts together across Excel and SQL — expert work, repetitive execution. The bottleneck wasn't intelligence; it was the manual plumbing between question and answer.

What I Did

Led the team that built and launched a hybrid AI agent for Finance — natural-language chat for ad-hoc analysis, paired with agentic workflows that autonomously execute multi-step tasks like variance analysis, forecast updates, and reporting. Partnered with Applied AI on the retrieval and tool-use architecture; drove the product strategy, user research, and rollout playbook.

Impact

Adopted by 150+ internal Walmart Finance users in its first year. Replaces fragmented Excel and SQL workflows with conversational and autonomous AI. Foundation for enterprise-wide FP&A transformation across the business.

02
2024 — Present ML Platform · Enterprise

Enterprise Forecasting Platform

Walmart's first unified ML forecasting system across Finance, Merchandising, and Supply Chain.

Problem

Finance and Merchandising were operating on different forecasting truths inside a $650B business — Finance modeling in Excel, Merchandising on legacy planning tools, no shared source of truth across the operating plan.

What I Did

Owned end-to-end product strategy for Walmart US's enterprise forecasting platform — driver-based models, ML demand forecasting, and Gen AI explainability — replacing fragmented workflows with a single planning system. Scaled the product organization from 4 to 15 PMs and presented quarterly to the C-suite.

Impact

Targeting 25 bps margin forecast accuracy improvement ($200M+ annual) and $400M incremental sales through reduced out-of-stocks. Platform vision adopted across six Merchandising business units (Food, Consumables, Hardlines, Fashion, Home, Entertainment).

2022 — 2024 ML Platform · Assortment

Assortment Optimization Platform

ML-enabled store-item optimization yielding 2% sales lift and $1B+ revenue potential.

Problem

Assortment decisions were stuck across competing SVP priorities — every business unit had its own goals, no one agreed on how to measure success, and ML was running in pockets without enterprise-wide impact.

What I Did

Aligned executive stakeholders on a shared sales-lift metric. Coordinated 60+ technical personnel across engineering, data science, and design. Partnered with Applied AI to validate ML models. Established product development practices that cut deployment cycles by 30%.

Impact

Delivered 2% sales lift with $1B+ annual revenue potential. Increased model accuracy by 30% and expanded business coverage 4x. Built the consensus that secured the $100M+ assortment transformation initiative.

How I Operate

I learned leadership on a nuclear submarine, technical depth at the Naval Academy, and strategy at Duke. I've spent the last decade applying that combination at Fortune 1 scale.

I don't ship features in search of a problem. I take ambiguous enterprise problems — Finance and Merchandising on different truths, ML running in pockets without business impact, Excel processes that won't scale — and turn them into platforms that change how the company operates. That requires both engineering credibility and the executive influence to fund and protect the work for years.
